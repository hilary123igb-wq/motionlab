Structural positional bias in British Parliamentary debating motions —

Every BP debate distributes exactly six team points across four positions, so a perfectly balanced position averages 1.50. Deviations from that baseline are measured here within each room, then averaged across the rooms that debated a given motion.

Because the comparison happens inside a single room, differences in bracket strength largely cancel. What remains is a mixture of genuine motion asymmetry and residual skill differences between the four teams drawn together — the latter is not yet controlled for.
